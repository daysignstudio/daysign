8th Wonder of the World

Invitation, Poster · 2026 · Celebrations

A single birthday invitation poster built around a travel theme — "my birthday, our adventure, let's celebrate together."

A vintage suitcase becomes the whole layout: the handle doubles as a header for the party details, stickers and tags carry the dress code and schedule, and a childhood photo peeks out from behind the case like an old travel memory.

Playful, a little chaotic, and printed at poster size to hang as decoration on the day itself rather than just live in an inbox.
