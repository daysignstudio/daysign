Business Card

Brand Identity, Print · 2026 · Corporate

Our own card had to do the one thing we ask every client's identity to do: say what the studio is, in one line, without a list that competes with itself.

The three divisions — Weddings, Celebrations, Corporate — sit as one sentence across the top of the card, closing on "design … studio" at the opposite corner. Printed on translucent vellum with a scattered confetti pattern underneath, so the card itself carries a bit of every occasion we design for.

The wordmark stays exactly as it lives everywhere else on the site — same mark, same weight — because the card is a business card, not a rebrand. A matching envelope carries the same confetti and the site's own line, "Isn't it a sign?", so the whole small stack of paper reads as one object.
