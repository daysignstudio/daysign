Anne & Cas

Wedding Identity, Illustration · 2026 · Weddings

A full day-of identity for Anne & Cas's wedding at the Red House — built around a warm, hand-drawn signage system that guided guests from ceremony to cake without a single generic arrow sign.

Every touchpoint got its own hand-lettered illustration: a bilingual welcome schedule (Danish and English, since the couple's families needed both), striped directional signs for the ceremony and bar, a seating chart with a hand-drawn table map, and a cake-table backdrop that doubled as a dessert menu.

The palette stayed playful and a little retro — red, green and blue on cream — echoing the striped tents and checkered tablecloths of the reception itself, so the print pieces felt like part of the party rather than instructions pinned to it.
