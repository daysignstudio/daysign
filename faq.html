FAQ.

Short answers to the questions we hear most — about prices, timing, printing and working with us in Copenhagen and London.

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@daysign.studio.

How much do custom wedding invitations cost in Copenhagen?

Custom design at Daysign starts from 2,500 DKK for a single piece, like an invitation, menu or welcome sign. A small set of matching pieces (The Essentials) starts from 12,000 DKK, and a complete wedding identity (The Full Day) from 25,000 DKK. Printing and production are quoted separately.

What does it cost in London?

The same packages in pounds: from £300 for one piece, from £1,400 for The Essentials and from £2,900 for The Full Day. Printing is quoted separately.

How far in advance should I order wedding stationery?

At least 2 months before your event, ideally around 6 months. That leaves time for design, revisions and printing without rushing.

Can you do a rush order?

Often, yes, depending on how busy the studio is. Rush projects may cost more, because your project moves to the front of the queue.

How long does it take to design one piece?

Usually from 2 days to about a week and a half, including revisions. It depends on the concept and how detailed the artwork is. For bigger packages, we agree on the timeline before we start.

How does the process work?

You tell us about your event. We propose a concept, design the pieces and refine them with you. Every package includes 2 rounds of revisions. Then we prepare print-ready files and can take care of printing for you.

Is printing included in the price?

No. Prices are for design. Printing depends on quantity, paper and finishing, so we quote it separately once the scope is clear. We work with trusted print partners and also produce small runs in-house.

Do you work in London and the UK?

Yes. Daysign works from Copenhagen and London. In London we find the right printer for your project and guide you on where and how to print it best.

Can you design in Danish, English or other languages?

Yes, we can design in any language: Danish, English, Russian or whatever your guests speak, including bilingual invitations.

What do you design for a julefrokost?

Most often a menu, place cards and an event poster. Everything is designed in one style, so the evening feels like your company's own party, not a template.

What can you design for a konfirmation?

Invitations, a menu, place cards, a welcome sign and a song sheet (sanghæfte). Choose one piece or a full matching set.

What kinds of events do you work on?

Weddings, birthdays, baby showers, christenings, konfirmation, dinner parties, company parties, julefrokost, product launches and brand events. If it's a day worth marking, we can design for it.

How do I get started?

Send us a short message through the form on the website or email hello@daysign.studio. Tell us the date, the type of event and roughly what you need, and we'll recommend the right starting point.
