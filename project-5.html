You're Invited to New Year

Invitation, Typography · 2026 · Celebrations

A digital invitation for Hari and Polly's New Year's Eve — designed to feel like a printed keepsake even though it only ever existed on a screen.

The whole piece rests on one big cursive line: "New Year" set in a warm, ornate script, printed in deep burgundy on warm cream stock — or at least, made to look and feel that way. Everything else is quiet around it: hosts' names up top, the details (December 31, 20:30) laid out simply below, with room left for the venue.

Guests were asked to bring one Christmas tree toy each — something small and personal — so the invitation needed to feel intimate rather than templated, closer to a letter than a flyer.
