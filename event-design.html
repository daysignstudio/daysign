Questions

What do you design for a julefrokost?

Most often a menu, place cards and an event poster — all in one style, so the evening feels like your company's own party, not a template.

Do you design and print for events in London?

Yes. Daysign works from Copenhagen and London. We design the pieces and find the right printer in London, or produce small runs ourselves.

Can you handle a tight deadline?

Often, yes, depending on the studio's schedule. Rush projects may cost more, because they move to the front of the queue.

How do we start?

Send us the date, the type of event and what you need — we'll recommend the right scope and send a quote.
